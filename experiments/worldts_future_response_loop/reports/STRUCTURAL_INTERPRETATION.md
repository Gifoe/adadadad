# Structural interpretation limits

The original causal condition encoder and causal decoder restrict a future condition at index s to outputs s through s+p_e+p_d-2. The full Jacobian audits establish this boundary for the actual checkpoints: NP has5 positions and PJM9, including the current position. This is a graph statement, not proof of a performance bottleneck.

The added temporal attention is already global after its **first** application. K2/K4 cannot extend its temporal reach beyond what K1 can access. Additional iterations change the nonlinear transformation and refine the correction using shared parameters; they may add representational or optimization effects. Any claim that K4 uniquely enables far-away information access would be incorrect for this implementation. Wide dilation provides a separate nonrecurrent pathway.

In the controlled AR system, the physical response is known. With a=.2 it is negligible beyond five steps; with a=.95 it remains large. Learned derivatives and forecasting errors are recorded separately. In the low-noise long-response fit, K4 has lower forecasting MSE than one-shot attention while its physical-Jacobian MSE is worse. Forecast accuracy alone therefore does not establish more faithful physical response recovery. These derivatives do not provide a causal interpretation on NP/PJM.

Zero final projection makes initial insertion equal to WorldTS in evaluation. After learning, Value-only correction and bias-free propagation preserve P(0)=0 for correction propagation. The bounded eta coefficient does **not** guarantee contraction or convergence, because the propagation operator's norm is unconstrained. Norm stability, iteration-to-iteration change and forecast quality must be inspected separately.

Full-state propagation is an explicit ablation: its Value sees b+delta, so it can introduce a response even when the original correction r is zero. Fixed anchoring is retained in that ablation to isolate the propagation object. The no-anchor alternative instead accumulates updates. All variants retain the same original decoder-stage freezing.

Known benchmark future conditions can be mixed bidirectionally without introducing future target labels. This differs from historical causal encoding. Deployment availability and covariate vintages are unverified, so a benchmark accuracy change does not establish a deployment benefit.

Stage1 jointly updates each model's encoder and predictor. Latent errors across separately trained models therefore refer to different learned spaces and cannot alone rank state quality. Within-checkpoint depth diagnostics keep the encoder fixed and have a more direct interpretation. The encoded-future reconstruction diagnostic is not an upper bound: a decoder may reconstruct encoded true states well while decoding off-manifold predicted states poorly.

The four unshared anchored blocks have an initialization caveat. All output projections start at zero; at the first backward pass only the last block's projection receives a gradient. In the NP H360 preflight, all four projection gradients and Q gradients are nonzero by the fifth step, but the first-block Q norm is3.08e-9 versus1.69e-3 in the last block. This delayed gradient flow is expected for the anchored composition, and differs from a residual stack with an identity derivative. Actual per-layer gradient norms and convergence histories are retained. A poor C3 score alone would not fully exclude an optimization explanation for the sharing contrast; the wider one-shot and cost comparisons are also needed.
